# 📊 KTM SHOWROOM SALES AND ANALYSIS DASHBOARD

## 🚀 Project Overview

- Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze KTM showroom sales data  
- Focused on transforming raw data into meaningful insights for business decision-making  
- Covered sales performance across cities, bike models, customer types, and payment methods  

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance  
- Track monthly revenue trends  
- Identify top-performing bike models  
- Understand customer behavior  
- Evaluate city-wise and sales executive performance  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop – Data visualization & dashboard creation  
- Power Query – Data cleaning (ETL process)  
- DAX – KPI calculations  
- SQL (MySQL) – Data analysis  

---

## 📊 Key Metrics (KPIs)

- Total Revenue: ₹946,080,000  
- Total Units Sold: 3,641  
- Total Orders: 1,200  
- Average Bike Price: ₹259,733  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- Monthly Revenue Trend Analysis  
- Units Sold by Bike Model  
- Revenue by City  
- Revenue by Sales Executive  
- Orders by Payment Method  
- Interactive filters (City, Bike Model, Customer Type)  

---

## 🧠 Key Insights

- Bangalore generates the highest revenue among all cities  
- Duke series bikes are the top-selling models  
- Revenue shows variation with noticeable monthly trends  
- Payment methods are evenly distributed across Card, Cash, and EMI  
- Both new and returning customers contribute to sales  

---

## 📂 Dataset Details

- ~1200+ records  
- Includes:  
  - City  
  - Bike Model  
  - Units Sold  
  - Revenue  
  - Customer Type  
  - Sales Executive  
  - Payment Mode  
  - Date
